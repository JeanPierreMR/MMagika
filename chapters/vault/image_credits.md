# Where the vault's pictures come from

All are photo textures from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com), released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication): free for any use, no credit required. Credited anyway.
They were downloaded at 1K resolution and turned into small WebP files.

| File | ambientCG asset | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `images/rusty_steel.webp` | [Metal063](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal063) | the vault door's steel |
| `images/heavy_rust.webp` | [Rust009](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rust009) | the thick rust creeping over the door's edges, and the handprint's grain |
| `images/concrete_wall.webp` | [Concrete042C](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete042C) | the wall around the door |

The red handprint is drawn by us (in vault.html), with the rust texture as its grain.
