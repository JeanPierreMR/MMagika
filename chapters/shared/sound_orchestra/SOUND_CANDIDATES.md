# Sound candidates

Real recordings found on Freesound that could replace the synthesized placeholders, one table row each.
**Nothing here is downloaded into the project.** The sound lab reads this file and lists every row in its cue's
"sound" dropdown under *candidates*, playing it straight from Freesound's preview so you can try it in place.

To adopt one:
1. Open its page, check the license there (they were found with Freesound's "Creative Commons 0" filter, but
   confirm), and download the original.
2. Upload it in the sound lab (or put it in `audio/`), pick it, press "copy", and paste the cue into `sound_book.js`.
3. Move its row into the credits table in `audio/README.md` (CC0 needs no credit, but it's kind to give one).

Previews only work in the lab: the site itself only plays files from its own folder (its security rules).

To add a candidate, add a row. The lab reads the columns in this order: **cue | title | author | license | page | preview**.
